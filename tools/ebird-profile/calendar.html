        Read this calendar

        Not hearing a bird does not establish that it is absent. Historical checklist frequency measures birds reported by people across different habitats; microphone detection depends on vocal activity, weather, recording uptime and model settings. A dot represents saved detections, including unverified identifications. Reviews marked false positive are excluded.

        Methods and limitations

        Historical rates use independent complete locality-days from the ten prior calendar years, smoothed over the selected week and its two neighboring weeks. Each window needs at least 100 checklists across three years. Arrival and departure markers describe sustained crossings of the larger of 2% reporting frequency or 20% of that species' annual maximum; peaks are periods at or above 80% of its maximum. These are estimates of seasonal reporting patterns, not precise migration dates. Seasons wrap across New Year. Common birds expected throughout the year are identified as year-round.

        Weeks begin January 1; week 52 includes December 31. February 29 shares February 28's seasonal bin. These are seasonal periods, not ISO week numbers. Weeks with no saved station detections have unknown microphone coverage, rather than assumed continuous listening. Repeated calls count toward total detections but each species counts at most once per day for the comparison.
